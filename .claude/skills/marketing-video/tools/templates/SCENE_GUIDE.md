## How a scene works

- One beat = one file, `compositions/<beat>.html`. You own **only your file**. Do
  not edit `index.html`, `captions.html`, `timeline.json` or another scene: they are
  generated or owned by someone else. A missing shared helper goes inside your file.
- The scene's slot length and word times come from the measured voice:
  `const B = window.MV.beat("B03")` gives `B.dur` and `B.words[k].s/.e` in the
  scene's local seconds. Land a punch-in, a reveal or a counter on the word that
  says it (`B.words[k].s`), never on a hand-typed number. Keep the fallback object
  the placeholder has, so the scene still opens on its own in the Studio.
- Hyperframes contract (full rules: the `hyperframes` skill, `references/format.md`):
  a paused GSAP timeline registered as `window.__timelines["<beat>"]`; `fromTo`, not
  `from`; no `Date.now()`, unseeded `Math.random()`, timers, network or `repeat: -1`;
  every visible thing is a function of timeline time; styles and scripts inside
  `<template>`; root styled as `#root`; ids prefixed with the beat (`#B03-card`);
  asset paths root-relative (`assets/media/…`).
- Delete `data-mvf="placeholder"` from your root when the scene is real. `mvf export`
  refuses a project that still has a placeholder.

## Look

- Brand tokens: background `{{BG}}`, text `{{FG}}`, accent `{{ACCENT}}`, muted `{{MUTED}}`
  (also `var(--mv-bg)`, `var(--mv-fg)`, `var(--mv-accent)`, `var(--mv-muted)`).
- Type: display `{{FONT_DISPLAY}}` 800, body `{{FONT_BODY}}`. On 9:16 a claim line
  is 88–120 px, nothing read is under 34 px, ≤ 5 words per line; cut words, never
  shrink.
- **Real product, never a mock-up.** UI on screen is a real screenshot or
  recording from `assets/media/` (captured from the product), shown at 1.6–2× its
  real size, pushed in on the one control that matters. A missing screen is a
  labelled placeholder and a line in your report, never a drawn dashboard.
- {{BRAND}} appears on screen as the product itself; the name is spoken at the CTA.

## Motion

- Something visible changes at least every 3.5 s: a cut inside the scene, a push,
  a reveal, a counter, a cursor click. No frame holds still longer than 2 s.
- Entrances 0.2–0.4 s (`power3.out`, `back.out(1.6)` for pops), exits shorter.
  The camera moves with intent: push in on the claim, pull back to reveal context.
- Transitions have a cause: the button becomes the next card, the swipe carries
  into the next scene. The last 0.2 s of your scene may hand off motion; the next
  scene's first frame must already be readable.
- Muted viewing is the default on X: the picture must tell the beat without sound.

## Check your scene

From the project directory:

```bash
npx hyperframes lint
npx hyperframes snapshot --at <t1>,<t2>,<t3>     # absolute times inside your slot (STORYBOARD.md)
```

Read the PNGs (`.hyperframes/artifacts/snapshots/`). Check the first and last
frame of the slot, the moment each word lands, and that nothing important sits in
the caption band or under the platform UI. Iterate until the scene is readable,
on-brand and alive. Report: what you built, the times you looked at, anything you
could not do (a missing screen, a fact you needed).
