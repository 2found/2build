## Standards (the checks hold every scene to these)
- **Own only your file:** `compositions/<beat>.html`. `index.html`,
  `captions.html`, `timeline.json` and the other scenes are generated or owned by
  someone else. A missing helper goes inside your file.
- **Timing from the voice.** `const B = window.MV.beat("B03")` gives `B.dur` and
  `B.words[k].s/.e` in local seconds. Anchor moments to words, never to typed
  numbers. Keep the placeholder's fallback object, so the scene opens alone.
- **The HyperFrames contract.** Full rules are in the `hyperframes` skill.
  - Register a paused GSAP timeline as `window.__timelines["<beat>"]`, and use
    `fromTo`, not `from`.
  - No `Date.now()`, unseeded `Math.random()`, timers, network or
    `repeat: -1`.
  - Everything visible is a function of timeline time.
  - A transform or filter the timeline animates is set by GSAP
    (`tl.set(sel, {rotation, scaleX}, 0)`), not in an inline `style`: GSAP
    reads the inline one as 0 and the render keeps it.
  - Styles and scripts go inside `<template>`, and the root is styled as
    `#root`.
  - Prefix ids with the beat (`#B03-card`), and keep asset paths
    root-relative (`assets/media/…`).
- **Real product.** UI is a real capture from `assets/media/`, never drawn. A
  missing screen is a labelled placeholder and a line in your report.
- **Approved art only.** Use `assets/art/<ID>.png` where the beat's `art` lists
  it, at no more than the size it was approved for. Words go in HTML over it,
  never in it.
- **Claims.** A number, superlative or "free" on screen traces to a fact in
  `01-product/facts.json`.
- **Zones.**

| Zone | y (px) | Keep |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Platform top bar | 0–{{SAFE_TOP}} | texture only |
| Title band | {{SAFE_TOP}}–~{{CAP_TOP}} | the on-screen line, the subject, the product |
| Caption band | {{CAP_TOP}}–{{CAP_BOTTOM}} | burned-in captions (generated): no faces or text |
| Platform UI | {{SAFE_BOTTOM}}–{{H}} | texture only |

  Side margins: {{SAFE_LEFT}} px on the left and {{SAFE_RIGHT}} px on the
  right.
- **Frame 0 of B01 is the cover.** It shows the subject and the hook line,
  never black, a logo or a title card.
- **Done means real.** Delete `data-mvf="placeholder"` from your root.
  `mvf export` refuses a project that still has one.

## Defaults (change them when the video calls for it)
- **Reuse the runtime if it helps.** Generated scenes run on `window.MVK`
  (`build`, `layer`, `rig`, `cam`, `rng`). A hand-written scene may call them,
  or use any HTML, SVG, canvas or GSAP of its own.
- **Brand tokens:** `var(--mv-bg)` {{BG}}, `var(--mv-fg)` {{FG}},
  `var(--mv-accent)` {{ACCENT}} and `var(--mv-muted)` {{MUTED}}. The display
  font is {{FONT_DISPLAY}} and the body font is {{FONT_BODY}}.
- **Readable on a phone:** few words per line and nothing small. Cut words
  rather than shrink them.
- **{{BRAND}}** is seen as the product itself where the value happens. A
  recipe that keeps the brand to the end (`"brand": "end"`) shows it once, at
  the CTA.
- **Build to the key frame** (`review/keyframe.jpg`, once it exists): the same
  place, light, figure size and lettering.
- **Something visibly changes every few seconds.** The picture tells the beat
  with the sound off.

## Check your scene
```bash
npx hyperframes lint
npx hyperframes snapshot --at <t1>,<t2>,<t3>     # absolute times inside your slot (STORYBOARD.md)
```
Lint reports `gsap_timeline_not_registered` once in `index.html`, at the
kit's `ghosts` helper. That timeline is driven by its layer's; leave it.
Look at the PNGs (`.hyperframes/artifacts/snapshots/`): the first and last
frames, each landing word, and the caption band. Report what you built, the
times you looked at, and anything you could not do.
